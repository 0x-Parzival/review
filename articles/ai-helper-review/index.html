AI Helper review: inbound calls, directed outbound calls, and summaries

AI Helper is a business phone agent. Its current site focuses on answering inbound calls, making outbound calls started by an authorized person, and giving the team a record of each conversation.

What AI Helper does

AI Helper uses business information and instructions to handle calls. The site describes a configurable greeting, language, voice, and call limits. After a conversation, teams can review transcripts, summaries, and follow-up notes. Recordings depend on whether the phone provider supplies them.

The current product domain is aihelper.si. Older references to aihelper.in should not be used as the product destination.

Inbound and outbound are different workflows

For inbound calls, prepare business hours, services, common questions, and clear boundaries for what the agent can answer. Decide how unresolved questions reach a person.

For outbound calls, an authorized person chooses the contact and starts each call with instructions. The public FAQ states that the agent does not initiate calls on its own. Keep the purpose specific and ensure the business has the necessary permission for the call.

What the published prices mean

On October 3, 2026, the public page listed $200 USD for inbound call handling and $500 USD for inbound plus outbound. It explicitly asks customers to confirm the billing interval, included call volume, and telephony charges before subscribing.

Those numbers should not be presented as an all-inclusive monthly price. Ask for the billing interval, country availability, provider costs, usage limits, and support scope in writing. Check the live pricing section for changes.

How to evaluate the demo

The public page offers a short voice or text conversation and a callback demo with consent. A short demonstration can show the interaction style; it cannot establish production reliability.

  1. Ask a normal question from your business.
  2. Ask something the agent should not know.
  3. Correct a detail and see how the conversation responds.
  4. Ask what happens when the caller needs a person.
  5. Confirm how summaries, retention, and access work in your setup.

This article is based on public product information. It is not a call-quality benchmark or a report of measured uptime.

Who should consider it?

Consider it for routine phone conversations where the business can provide clear information and review outcomes. Complex disputes, sensitive advice, and exceptions need a defined human process. The agent should identify itself as AI; the current site says default greetings do so.
